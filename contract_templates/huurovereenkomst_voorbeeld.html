Article 2 - Duration of the Rental Agreement

The rental agreement starts on {{ ingangsdatum }}{% if einddatum %}, and ends on {{ einddatum }}. Both parties explicitly declare that this is a temporary contract and will not be automatically extended.{% else %} for an indefinite period.{% endif %}

{% if einddatum %}

Termination confirmation: The landlord will inform the tenant in writing by no later than 1 - 3 months before the end date that the agreement will definitively end on {{ einddatum }}. If the tenant does not vacate the premises voluntarily after this date, they will be unlawfully occupying the property, and the landlord may take legal action. Extension of the agreement is not possible.

{% endif %}

Dissolutive Condition (Permit): This agreement is entered into under the express condition that the landlord holds a valid room-rental permit (kamerverhuurvergunning). In the event that this permit is revoked or denied by the municipality due to local objections or policy changes, this rental agreement will be dissolved (ontbonden) by operation of law without any compensation being due to the tenant.

Early termination: The tenant may terminate the agreement early with a written notice period of one full calendar month.

Landlord Termination: The landlord may only terminate the agreement in the following cases:

  • Rent arrears exceeding 4 weeks.
  • Serious violation of the rental agreement (e.g., subletting or severe damage to the property).
  • Violation of the House Rules and Conduct as specified in Article 9 (Zero Tolerance Policy).

The landlord must comply with Dutch rental law when terminating the agreement.

Article 3 - Student Obligation

The tenant must be a student at the start and throughout the entire rental period. The landlord has the right to request proof of enrollment annually. The tenant must provide valid proof of enrollment by email to the landlord within three months of the request. If the tenant discontinues their studies, they must inform the landlord immediately. If the tenant fails to provide proof of enrollment, the landlord has the right to terminate the agreement with one month's notice. This student clause does not affect the temporary nature of the tenancy agreement as referred to in Article 7:271 paragraph 1 of the Dutch Civil Code.

Article 5 - Payment Terms & Consequences of Late Payment

If the rent is more than 7 days late, the tenant will receive a written warning. If the rent remains unpaid for more than 4 weeks, the landlord may initiate legal eviction proceedings under Article 7:231 of the Dutch Civil Code. The landlord may not unilaterally terminate the agreement without legal action.

Annual settlement: If actual service charges are lower than the advance payment, the tenant will receive a refund. If costs are higher, the tenant may owe an additional amount. Service charges are divided equally among the {{ aantal_bewoners }} tenant{% if aantal_bewoners != 1 %}s{% endif %} at the address, regardless of individual usage.

Article 8 - Municipal Taxes

The tenant is personally responsible for their share of municipal tax assessments (user's share). This may require the tenant to advance the payment and settle with the other tenants. The landlord is not responsible for the payment, coordination, or settlement of these taxes. The owner's share (OZB) remains the responsibility of the landlord.

Article 9 - Use, House Rules and Conduct

  • Subletting: Strictly prohibited.
  • Common Areas: No personal items or belongings may be stored in shared hallways or common areas.
  • Nuisance: No nuisance to the neighborhood (noise, littering, etc.).
  • Visitors: Receiving visitors/guests for longer than 2 days is not permitted without permission of the landlord.
  • Quiet Hours: Strict quiet hours apply in the house and neighborhood from 22:00 to 06:00 hours.
  • Substances: Smoking and the use/possession of drugs are entirely prohibited inside and outside the building. Excessive alcohol consumption is not allowed.
  • Zero Tolerance: The landlord maintains a zero-tolerance policy. Any violation of these rules constitutes a material breach and grounded reason to initiate immediate dissolution (ontbinding) of the contract.

Article 10 - Maintenance and Handover

The tenant must keep the room clean and in good condition. Upon termination, the room must be handed over in its original state and cleaned thoroughly. Any belongings left behind will be removed at the tenant's expense. Shared areas must be kept clean by mutual agreement.

Article 11 - Applicable Law

This contract is governed by Dutch law. If any provision is declared invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

Article 13 - Maintenance and Repairs

Maintenance by the tenant: responsible for keeping the room/shared spaces clean; unclogging drains (kitchen/bath/shower) caused by grease, food, or hair; replacing batteries in smart radiator thermostats and smoke detectors; preventing moisture and mold via adequate ventilation/heating; replacing bulbs and fuses.

Maintenance by the landlord: responsible for structural issues (leaks/heating) and shared equipment repair (fridge/washer/dishwasher).

Reporting: defects must be reported timely. Damage caused by negligence or failure to ventilate will be charged to the tenant.

Article 14 - Landlord's Access to the Property

The landlord has the right to enter for maintenance, inspections, or viewings with an appointment made at least 24 hours in advance, except in emergencies.

Article 15 - Insurance

The tenant is responsible for contents insurance. The landlord holds building and liability insurance.
